# Logs

One short log per working session, drafted by the agent at the end of the session and
corrected by you. Four fields. It should take under two minutes.

**Why it exists:** the trial reports on itself using the tool being trialled. Reflection
journals normally die by week three because nobody fills them in; here the agent writes the
draft and you fix what it got wrong, which produces evidence that would otherwise never be
collected. At the end of the year the evaluation is built from these logs rather than from
anyone's impression of how it went.

**Corrections are the valuable part.** A log you rubber-stamped tells us less than one you
argued with. Where the agent got it wrong, or wasted your time, or produced something
unusable — leave that in.

**These are yours.** Nothing syncs to anyone. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it,
session by session. The agent will ask once at the end of each wrap-up and won't ask twice.

**Nothing identifying goes in a log** — no student names, no details that would identify a
student or a colleague. See `../Context/boundaries.md`.

Escalation notes the agent writes when it can't solve something also land here, as
`escalation-<date>.md`. Those are drafts for you to send, or not.

To run a wrap-up, just say "wrap up" at the end of a session.
